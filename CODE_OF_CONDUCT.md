# Code of conduct

Streamcase follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Demonstrate empathy and kindness.
- Respect differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Take responsibility, apologize, and learn from mistakes.
- Focus on what is best for the community and project.

Harassment, personal attacks, sexualized conduct, trolling, and publication of
another person's private information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer at
`sepurisaikrishna55@gmail.com`. Reports will be reviewed promptly and kept
confidential as far as reasonably possible. Maintainers may issue corrections,
warnings, temporary bans, or permanent bans depending on impact and pattern.
